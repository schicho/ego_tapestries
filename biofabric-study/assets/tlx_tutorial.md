# Workload questionnaire

After each group of questions, you will rate how demanding the questions were, using six sliders: mental demand, physical demand, temporal demand, performance, effort and frustration. Move each slider to the position that matches your experience.

<img alt="The six sliders of the workload questionnaire. The Performance slider goes from Good on the left to Poor on the right, the others from Low to High" src="biofabric-study/assets/images/tlx-sliders.png" width="60%" mx="auto" />

<span style="color: #e03131; font-weight: 600;">Pay attention: the **Performance** slider is the other way around. On the other sliders, the right end means *more* (high demand, high effort), but on the Performance slider the left end is **Good** and the right end is **Poor**.</span>
